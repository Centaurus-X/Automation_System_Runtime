# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want a technically rigorous, respectful environment in which contributors
can disagree constructively. Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence, behavior and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- respect different levels of experience and domain knowledge;
- communicate safety and security concerns clearly and without sensationalism;
- accept correction, disclose uncertainty and avoid unsupported claims;
- protect confidential, customer and plant information;
- keep issues and reviews focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sexualized
conduct and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions
should consider context, impact, prior behavior and the safety of the community.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through an available
private GitHub channel. Do not use a public issue when a report contains private
or identifying information. Immediate threats or platform abuse should also be
reported through GitHub's abuse-reporting mechanisms.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public community spaces.
